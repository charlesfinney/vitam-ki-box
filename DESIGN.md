# Vita Concierge: what premium means for our buyers

Nov 8, 2026 · Charles Finney +1 (332) 249-3276

Draft for Charles. Every visual change to the site is judged against this page. Where it and a pasted decision from the claude.ai Project disagree, the Project wins (CLAUDE.md, rule 10).

## Who is looking
1. Lawyers, doctors, tax advisers and owners of small firms in Germany. They keep other people's secrets and are liable for them.
2. Investors, friends and partners in Germany, the US and Ukraine, who judge whether we are serious.
3. They judge a supplier the way they judge a notary or a private bank: by care, by restraint, and by who answers the phone.

## Premium, for them, means
1. **Calm, not loud.** Quiet pages, few colours, generous space. One moving moment per page (the demo). Nothing glows, nothing plays by itself, nothing competes for attention.
2. **Discreet.** Say less, prove more. What Vita leaves out (Wi-Fi, account, cloud) is shown as a feature.
3. **Precise.** Real figures with sources. Provisional prices marked. Illustrations labelled as illustrations. Numbers set in tabular figures, headings set tight.
4. **Accountable.** People with names, faces and phone numbers. The worst case spelled out before the sale. The service book, signed at every visit.
5. **Made by hand where it counts.** Our own photos of the real case and the real service book, a hand-drawn mark. No stock images, no AI-generated images, no other company's product shots.

## Not us
Startup gloss: gradients, glass, glow, 3D blobs, neon. "AI magic" sparkles and brains. Mascots and playful illustration. The dark hacker look. Fintech friendliness. Statistics we can't source.

## The visual world (as built, Nov 8, 2026)
1. Black top bar, white header, white and lavender (#e6e6f0) sections, slate (#424257) bands.
2. Colourful line icons in one stroke weight: #d4800f, #5aa31c, #2b9bd9, #4a69c9, #c9425a.
3. Type: Unbounded (light) for headings, big numbers and the wordmark; Inter for all other text. Both self-hosted, Latin and Cyrillic.
4. Photography (to come): the real case in daylight on a neutral surface, open and closed, at a desk. Hands at work, no staged faces. The service book page with stamp and signature.

## The one thing only we would do
The service book: a paper record, stamped and signed by a person at every visit. It is our proof of care, and it should become the recurring visual motif (stamp, signature line, entries), first as real scans, later in the logo and the share cards.

## Before anything ships
1. Would a careful law firm put this on its desk?
2. In five seconds, does a new visitor know what it is, who it is for, and why to trust it?
3. Name one decision in the change that only Vita Concierge would make.
4. Checks pass: contrast (WCAG AA), 390 and 1280 px without overflow, no request leaving the site.
