Models want to be free.

Your Vita runs open models: freely available, free of nannying, and once delivered they learn only from your data.

  • Open and clean

    The building blocks of open models are out in the open. Anyone can check what they can do, with no telemetry and no back door. Trust you can verify is worth more than trust you have to take on faith.

  • No nannying

    We remove the makers’ blanket refusals; the technical term is “abliteration.” A defense attorney has to be able to talk about crimes, a doctor about overdoses. A tool for adults treats you like one.

  • Independent

    No corporation and no government decides what your AI may discuss with you. Whatever it learns, it learns from you alone.

Freedom comes with responsibility: what you do with your AI is your call, and yours to answer for.

Questions

Does my data really stay with me?

Yes. Vita has no internet access. Your data lives in your case and nowhere else. Not even we can get in from outside.

Which AI models does it run?

Open models we pick for Vita, test, and free of blanket refusals. After delivery, they learn only from your data.

How does it stay current without internet?

In the first year we come by every month and install the latest version, in the morning and with a text 45 minutes ahead. You can watch: none of your files leaves the case, and afterwards the update is in your service book. After the first year, an optional service plan, billed once a year, keeps the visits coming. Vita keeps working fully without it.

Do the camera and microphone listen in?

Only when you switch them on. Each has its own switch that cuts the power, and a light that stays on while it’s on.

Do I need technical know-how?

No. We set everything up and show you how it works. After that: open it and ask.

Can several people use it?

Yes, one after another, right at the case. For several people working at the same time, you’ll need several cases.

64 or 128 GB?

64 GB is plenty for everyday work. 128 GB pays off if several tasks should run at once. Memory can’t be upgraded later.

What do I tell my data protection officer?

That your data doesn’t leave your premises, because Vita has no connection to the outside world. We give you an info sheet to pass alongRory Sutherland: when people buy something new at work, above all they don’t want to be blamed. “Nobody ever got fired for buying IBM.” The info sheet is your cover., and the service book documents every update. Whether your use fits your professional rules is a question for your bar association or professional body.

What if it’s not right for me?

As a consumer you can withdraw within 14 days at no cost; we refund the full price including shipping, and you pay only for the return. Up to day 30 we take Vita back from any customer voluntarily, minus 15% for configuration and testing.

And if something breaks?

Your statutory warranty rights apply. On top of that, the first buyer gets a one-year warranty from us, and we take care of repair or replacement. Your storage drive stays with you.
