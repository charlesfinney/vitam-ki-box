How we measure.

An AI you can’t check is an AI you have to take on faith. Vita you should be able to check: every update on your own tasks, every figure with its method in the open.

A test bench in your case.

At setup, we put together about 20 tasks from your own work with you: a brief to summarize, a deadline, a lab report. They stay in the case, like everything else.

  1. 1

    Before every update

    The new version answers each of your tasks 25 times, and so does the current one. We check that facts, deadlines and amounts are right and nothing is made up.

  2. 2

    Only if it does no worse

    An update goes in only if it does no worse on average and slips on no critical check, such as a deadline or an emergency warning. Otherwise your current AI stays.

  3. 3

    In black and white

    The result goes into your service book, signed. So you can see your AI getting better, and never quietly getting worse.

The open Vita test.

How good are open models at German legal, medical and tax work? So far there is no open test for that. We’re building one and publishing everything: tasks, checks, code and results.

  • 15 tasks

    Version 0.1: five each from law firms, medical practices and tax advisers. Written on invented documents, never on real files.

  • 86 checks

    Deadlines, amounts, diagnoses, and what must not be made up.

  • 25 answers per task

    5 runs × 5 answers. Answers vary, so we report the average with its margin of error.

  • Speed from the text

    Time to the first word, words per second, reading speed.

Every task has a correct model answer and a typical wrong one. An automatic test makes sure the checks accept the right one and catch the wrong one.

Before we publish results, practitioners from each field review the tasks. First results follow with the first series, measured on Vita’s own hardware.

Three settings, chosen by measurement.

You only choose how you want to work. Which model sits behind each setting is decided on the test bench.

  • Fast

    The fastest model within 10 points of the best. For dictation and quick questions.

  • Balanced

    Quality counts twice as much as speed. For everyday work.

  • Thorough

    The model with the best results, even if it takes longer. For deadlines, lab reports and tax notices.

What we don’t do.

  • None of your tasks leaves the case, not even for testing.
  • No figure without a measurement: until the first series, we state targets, not results.
  • No polished tables: we publish what does badly, too.
